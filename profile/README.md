# Shena Agency 🦋

We build web products for businesses in Egypt and the region: multi-tenant SaaS platforms,
clinic and booking systems, CRMs and messaging tools. Everything we ship is bilingual
(**English / العربية**) and built to run reliably on real-world hosting.

---

## What we build

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Wasla — وصلة** | Multi-tenant e-commerce platform. Each store gets a themeable storefront, a store builder and landing pages. It handles online payments and cash on delivery, coupons, inventory, and **Bosta** shipping with live tracking. |
| **MediCare** | Clinic management and online booking for clinics with multiple branches. It covers visits and calendars (including a dental chart), patients, doctors and commissions, finances, inventory, reports, and WhatsApp reminders. |
| **ChatBridge** | WhatsApp API gateway. Link a number by QR code, then send and receive messages through a simple REST API and webhooks. |
| **NexBot** | AI customer-service chatbots trained on your own documents, answering on WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram and your website, with hand-off to human agents. |

We also deliver **custom client work**: clinic websites connected to MediCare booking, operations
CRMs for tourism companies, and internal team-management tools.
